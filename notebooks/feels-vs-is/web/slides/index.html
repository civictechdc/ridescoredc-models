Hidden danger

The safest-looking streets on the stress map carry some of the worst crash records.

  • 14th St NW protected lane, LTS 1, comfort 93: 9 injury crashes on one block, 1 serious.
  • 9th St NW two-way track at Rhode Island Ave: 7 injury crashes at the junction against 0.5 expected for its design. Worst intersection in the city.
  • 15th St NW track: 55 injury crashes, 4.5× the citywide midblock rate, crossed by up to 50 streets per km.
  • 21st and I St NW, two blocks from this room: 3 injury crashes within 120 m. The July 2022 truck death is not recorded as a bicyclist fatality in the layer.

Reports, trend, and changes on the ground

Search any street or ward for a report: trend since 2025, worst blocks, and what DDOT built there.

  • Trend relative to the citywide 1.7× rise in recorded injuries. 15th St: flat at 1.0×. 9th St: 1.5× the city, just under the rising line.
  • DDOT ProTrack projects matched to blocks, with crash rates before and after completion. 340 projects, 205 km.
  • 9th St NW track, completed Jan 2023: 4.8 → 12.6 injury crashes a year. No exposure data, so part of that is more riders.
  • What it can't tell you: bicycle exposure. Low harm can mean few riders.
